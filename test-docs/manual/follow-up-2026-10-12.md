## Native-select investigation

Initial Chromium FOLLOWUP-14 expected Tab to focus Clear controls after `Home → ArrowDown → Enter`. It stopped with focus still on Genre; Firefox passed. The [first run](evidence/follow-up-2026-10-12/first-run/results.json) retains the stack and screenshot.

A [small probe](evidence/follow-up-2026-10-12/keyboard-select-probe.json) compared three sequences in both browsers. In Chromium, Escape before Tab or omitting unnecessary Enter moved focus to Clear controls. Firefox moved there for all three. This isolates a native-select sequence difference on this host; app controls/listeners were unchanged.

The checker now uses `Home → ArrowDown → Tab` for a closed select. Only the two keyboard groups were rerun; both passed. [Pre-correction checker](evidence/follow-up-2026-10-12/keyboard-before-correction.mjs) and [corrected checker](evidence/follow-up-2026-10-12/run-checks.mjs) are retained.

Counts across the two main runs: **19 group executions, 18 passes, one corrected harness failure; 17 distinct groups with passing latest results and zero unresolved failures.** This is not 19 distinct tests or one uninterrupted 17/17 passing run. No product defect/fix is claimed for the key sequence.
